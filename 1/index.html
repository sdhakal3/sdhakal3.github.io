Isaac Moriah

Issac Moriah is the main character of the critically acclaimed 2014 rougelike video game The Binding of Isaac: Rebirth.

Reasons I admire this character:

  • Even though Isaac is in a state of perpetual weeping throughout the course of the game, he never stops moving forward.
  • Issac has a vivid imagination even when he's in a terrible state.
  • Issac chooses his own fate. Or atleast he thinks he does, which is an inspiring thinking process.
