Project 2 - Web Application Proposal

Topic Description

The purpose of this proposal is to provide a general overview of my topic for project 2 in the CSCI3054 (Web Programming) class. The topic I’ve chosen is The Orchestra (Music Theory). I’ve taken music theory in college, and I’ve found that it’s an interesting but underappreciated science for people outside of the music making sphere. Knowing more about music theory lets a person look at the art of music and music making from another perspective. It lets them appreciate the practice of music and the thought that went into making it. I believe that showcasing the importance of music theory is best done by providing insight into how a beautiful ensemble of different sounds and rhythms comes to create a cohesive and cathartic piece in the form of an orchestra. How do multiple people play different instruments that amplify each other instead of overriding them? How do they practice getting into such a hivemind-like state where everyone is playing the same beat down to a fraction of a second? What does the person with the wand at the very front of an orchestra do, and are they important at all? All of these questions that the average person has about the orchestra can be answered using theory.

There have been attempts to provide more information about music theory and orchestration to the general public. However, I’ve found that most attempts are either too surface-level (The Kennedy Center) or too in-depth (Viva's Pressbooks) for an average person that knows nothing about music theory to make use of. What this website will try and do is bridge that gap between the average and professional so that there’s more appreciation for the art of music making within the general public.

App Description

An application description of a potential web application that can be used to address orchestration and music theory (a proposed "solution"):

  1. Page 1: On the home page, I plan to have a paragraph or multiple paragraphs describing what music theory and orchestration are. On this page, there will also be some images, for example, an image of sheet music, an orchestra, the instruments, and also some music theory notations.
  2. Page 2: The user will be able to do two different actions in a sequence. The first four choices of the two-step sequence will be picking one instrument family, such as strings, woodwinds, brass, and percussion. Then, the second four choices will display four different instruments belonging to that family. After both choices, the page will show detailed information about the selected instrument. The information will be a photograph(s) of the instrument, music theory data (such as its lowest/highest note, what clef it uses, its range, etc.), how it actually produces sound, where they are usually seated on an orchestra, and a popular orchestra piece that features the specific instrument. Here is an example of how the choices will work:
    1. Strings
      1. Violin
      2. Viola
      3. Cello
      4. Double Bass
    3. Woodwinds
      1. Flute
      2. Clarinet
      3. Oboe
      4. Saxophone
    5. Brass
      1. Trombone
      2. Tuba
      3. Cornet
      4. Flugelhorn
    7. Percussion
      1. Bass Drum
      2. Triangle
      3. Xylophone
      4. Timpani
  3. Page 3: The third page will consist of data on the basics of music theory that will tie in and provide some reference of the instrument data. It will also show how notes relate to each other using a simple graphic, among other things. An example of a reference to be used for this step is musictheory.net.
  4. Page 4: From the contact us page, I intend to collect user opinions on how the site could be improved and also their experiences on how the site helped them learn.
